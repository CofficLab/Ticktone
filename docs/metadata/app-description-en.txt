Ticktone - Precise Metronome for Practice

A precise, native metronome for macOS and iOS. Ticktone runs entirely on-device: no account, no network, no analytics — just a steady click you can trust.

Key Features
• Sample-accurate engine — a dedicated audio render path keeps the click steady even under UI load
• Tempo 30–300 BPM with Italian tempo markings, tap tempo, and tempo lock
• Time signatures and subdivisions — beats per measure, beat unit, duple / triple / quadruple subdivisions
• Accent patterns — cycle weak / normal / accent / mute for each beat
• Practice tools — count-in measures, tempo training with target BPM and step size, visual flash, and haptics
• Presets — save and recall complete configurations
• Pendulum and beat indicators that follow your configured pattern
• Three click sound sets (Classic, Electronic, Wood) with adjustable volume

Why Ticktone?

Many metronomes drift when the screen gets busy, or bury simple practice tools behind accounts and subscriptions. Ticktone keeps the timing engine on a dedicated audio path, so the click stays rock-solid while you scroll, switch presets, or train tempo. Everything is included from the first launch — no sign-in, no ads, no tracking.

• Instrumentalists building steady time — piano, guitar, violin, drums, and more
• Students practicing with a teacher's tempo assignments
• Teachers demonstrating rhythm patterns with accents and subdivisions
• Anyone who wants a clean, native metronome that works offline
